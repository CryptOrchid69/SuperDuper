Welcome to the SuperDuper INFO-CENTER.

Use the arrow beside a folder to reveal its contents.

Right click a folder to create a new folder, paste copied folders or text
documents, rename an ordinary folder, refresh the view, or open the selected
location in Windows File Explorer.

Double click any visible text document to have APP Voice read it aloud.

The main INFO-CENTER folder is protected and cannot be renamed or deleted.
Folders and text documents placed inside it remain editable.
